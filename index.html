The Band Chicago!

Chicago is an American rock band founded in 1967. Proudly calling themselves a "rock and roll band with horns", the group is famous for blending traditional rock with elements of jazz, classical music, R&B, and pop. (Click Picture for Video)

Recalling the band's formation, original drummer Danny Seraphine wanted to assemble skilled musicians and a horn section that blended rock and jazz.

My sister and I experienced this amazing concert on Tuesday, 8/5/25!

We had an amazing time at Musikfest in Bethlehem, PA, watching Chicago rock the outdoor stage. Lead singer Neil Donell and the band were incredible in the videos above and below! 😎

Chicago's early albums blended complex arrangements and stellar musicianship with a gritty edge, anchored by original guitarist Terry Kath—whom Jimi Hendrix famously called one of the best alive.
